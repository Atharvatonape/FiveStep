📖 How the two-story joke works

    

      
Greg Dean's model: every joke is two stories sharing one connector. The audience believes the 1st Story; the punch flips it into the 2nd.

      
SetupThe opening line. Steer the audience toward one obvious reading.

      
1st StoryWhat the audience assumes is happening.

      
Target AssumptionThe specific belief you're about to break — "What are they sure this means?"

      
ConnectorThe word or phrase that honestly carries two meanings. The hinge of the joke.

      
ReinterpretationThe second, hidden meaning of the connector.

      
2nd StoryThe surprising true story the reinterpretation reveals.

      
PunchDelivers the 2nd Story as late and as briefly as possible.

      
💡 Write the Connector first. If your setup and punch don't share one, the joke has no hinge.
